Our story

Built because everything became a subscription.

Today, almost every service quietly renews itself each month, from streaming and software to cloud storage and delivery apps. It’s easy to lose track of what you’re paying for, when it renews, or how much it adds up to.

  • Streaming
  • Software
  • Cloud storage
  • Delivery apps

Riyal was built by two developers to fix that. It automatically detects recurring payments, sorts them into Subscriptions, Bills, and People (household staff and allowances), and gives you one place to see where your money goes each month, in a bilingual Arabic/English experience designed around how people in Saudi Arabia actually track money.

What is Riyal?

One place for everything that recurs.

Riyal is a bilingual financial management app that brings recurring payments, subscriptions, bills, and payments to people into one organized experience.

ENع

Fully bilingual, native Arabic (RTL) and English, not a translated afterthought.

Three kinds of recurring payments

  • Subscriptions

    Streaming, software, cloud storage and other monthly renewals.

  • Bills

    Electricity, water, internet, phone bills and more.

  • People

    Household staff and allowances.

Each category has its own budget.

Features

Designed to make spending clear.

  • Automatic detection

    Spot a merchant charging 4+ times and Riyal classifies it as a recurring payment for you, no manual entry needed.

    Subscriptions

  • Budget monitoring

    Set a limit per category. Progress bars turn orange, then red, as you approach it.

    Within budget
    Approaching
    Reached

  • Spending analytics

    Total and category-level breakdowns, plus trends over time, by week, month or year.

  • Free-trial tracking

    Get reminded 5 days before a free trial converts to a paid plan.

  • Monthly money check-in

    Review each subscription with questions like “Are you still using it?” and “Will you need it over the next year?” to decide what to keep or cancel.

  • Riyal AI

    A Gemini-powered assistant that answers budgeting questions in Arabic or English, scoped to your spending, no invented numbers, no fake actions.

The project

A student project, built like a real product.

Riyal was developed as a student project. Building it meant solving the hard parts of a finance app: finding patterns in transactions, designing data that could grow with the project, and making one interface feel native in both Arabic and English.

Banking data in Riyal is simulated for this student project. Riyal does not connect to real bank accounts.

  • Pattern detection

    A simulated transaction feed is analysed, and a repeating merchant is classified into the right category automatically.

  • A backend that evolved

    A Supabase Postgres schema shaped through SQL migrations, from an early bank-integration experiment to the simulated banking model Riyal uses today.

  • Bilingual from the start

    One interface, native in Arabic (RTL) and English.
